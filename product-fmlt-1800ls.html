80GHz FMCW Radar Level Transmitter (Solids & Liquids)

The FMLT-1800LS is an advanced 76GHz–81GHz Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) non-contact radar level transmitter. Featuring a 5GHz scanning bandwidth and an ultra-narrow 3° beam angle, it delivers millimeter-level accuracy (±1mm) across ranges up to 120 meters. It penetrates dense dust, vapor, foam, condensation, and extreme temperatures (+200°C), making it the premier continuous level measurement solution for storage silos, tall process vessels, and aggressive chemical tanks.

Working Principle of 80GHz FMCW Radar Level Transmitter (Solids & Liquids)

The FMLT-1800LS emits a continuous high-frequency electromagnetic radar wave whose frequency varies linearly from 76GHz to 81GHz (FMCW).

The transmitted microwave signal travels at the speed of light, reflects off the surface of the measured medium, and is captured by the high-gain lens antenna.

Because the frequency changes continuously over time, the received echo frequency differs from the currently emitted frequency. The internal DSP mixer computes the beat frequency difference, which is directly proportional to the distance to the product surface, calculating exact continuous level.
