Vibrating Rod Level Switch

The VRLS-500S is a single-rod vibrating level switch that completely eliminates material bridging, jamming, and false alarms between fork tines. Ideal for granular materials, plastic flakes, wood chips, and agricultural grains.

Applications & Suitability

Plastic Flakes & Regrind: Level detection in recycling plants where flakes would jam standard forks.

Wood Pellets & Biomass: Silo level switching in biomass and sawmills.

Animal Feed & Grains: Immune to grain lodging and corn husks.

The VRLS-500S is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Single vibrating rod design completely prevents material bridging.
  • Self-cleaning vibration sheds loose product buildup.
  • No calibration required across different bulk densities.
  • Universal AC/DC power supply.

Working Principle of Vibrating Rod Level Switch

Single stainless steel rod vibrates at resonance. When product covers the rod, vibration stops, triggering switching.
