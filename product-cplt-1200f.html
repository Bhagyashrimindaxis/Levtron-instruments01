Working Principle of Continuous Fuel Level Transmitter (Diesel, Petrol, Fuels & Solvents)

The CPLT-1200F measures the change in capacitance between a central sensing rod and the outer concentric grounding tube as fuel enters the annular gap.

Because hydrocarbon fuels have a specific dielectric constant (εr ≈ 2.0 to 2.4) compared to air (εr = 1.0), the electrical capacitance varies linearly with the liquid column height.

Advanced digital filtering suppresses sloshing noise, producing an exceptionally smooth 4-20mA signal proportional to fuel depth.
