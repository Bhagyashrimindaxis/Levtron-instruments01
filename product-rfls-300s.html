Working Principle of Standard RF Admittance Level Switch

The RFLS-300S measures admittance (conductance and capacitance) at radio frequency using a 3-element probe: Sense, Shield, and Earth.

A high-frequency sine wave is applied equally to both the sense electrode and the compensation shield. Because the shield and sense voltages are identical, zero current flows between them through any coating.

Current flows only when the process medium bridges between the sense probe and the grounded vessel wall, guaranteeing 100% false-alarm-free detection even under extreme coating.
