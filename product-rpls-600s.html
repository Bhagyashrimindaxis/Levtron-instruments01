Rotating Paddle Level Switch

The RPLS-600S is an electro-mechanical rotary paddle level switch designed for bulk solids in silos and hoppers. It is available with multiple interchangeable paddle blade designs (1, 2, 3, 4 Vane, Scythe / T-Type, and J-Type) for various material densities.

Applications & Suitability

Grain & Flour Silos: Level detection in food processing silos, animal feed bins, and grain elevators.

Cement & Sand Hoppers: High and low level monitoring in concrete batching plants.

Plastic Pellets & Regrind: Level alarm in compounding tanks and injection molding hoppers.

The RPLS-600S is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Synchronous geared motor drives the paddle at 1 RPM.
  • Motor automatically cuts power when stalled by material, extending motor lifespan.
  • Multiple paddle designs: 1-Vane (cross-nozzle), 2-Vane, 3-Vane, 4-Vane, Scythe / T-Type, and J-Type.
  • Adjustable spring tension for light to heavy bulk densities.
  • Weatherproof IP65 / IP66 enclosure.

Working Principle of Rotating Paddle Level Switch

A low-speed motor slowly rotates the paddle in free air. When rising solid material arrests the paddle rotation, the motor swings on its spring mount to activate a microswitch, signaling the alarm and turning off the motor.
