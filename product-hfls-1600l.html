Side Mounted Magnetic Float Switch

The HFLS-1600L is a horizontal side-mounted magnetic float switch featuring glandless magnetic coupling for liquid tanks, boilers, and lubricating reservoirs. The magnetic coupling completely isolates process fluid from the electrical switch compartment.

Applications & Suitability

Boiler Water Level: High and low water alarm in steam boilers and condensate tanks.

Fuel & Lube Oil Tanks: Reliable level switching in oil reservoirs.

Chemical Process Vessels: Leak-proof level control in pressurized chemical vessels.

The HFLS-1600L is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Glandless magnetic coupling ensures zero fluid leakage into switch housing.
  • High temperature and pressure ratings (up to 250°C and 25 Bar).
  • Stainless steel SS316 float and wetted body.
  • Weatherproof IP66 or Flameproof Exd enclosure.

Working Principle of Side Mounted Magnetic Float Switch

A float on a pivoted arm moves with liquid level. A magnet on the float arm acts through a solid non-magnetic partition wall to repel an internal switch magnet, snapping the microswitch.
