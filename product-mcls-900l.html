Minicap Capacitive Level Sensor

The MCLS-900L is an ultra-compact tip-sensitive capacitive level sensor designed for space-constrained industrial machinery, lubrication tanks, chemical dosing systems, and semiconductor equipment.

Applications & Suitability

Lube Oil Systems: Low oil alarm in machine tool lubrication reservoirs.

Chemical Dosing: Level detection in small reagent bottles and dosing vessels.

Semiconductor Equipment: High-purity chemical and DI water level monitoring.

The MCLS-900L is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Tip-sensitive design ignores side wall proximity.
  • Miniature threaded body (M18 / M30) for compact mounting.
  • Fast response time (< 50 ms).
  • Direct PNP/NPN DC transistor output.

Working Principle of Minicap Capacitive Level Sensor

An AC electric signal generated at the tip of the sensing electrode changes capacitance when immersed in medium, converting to a switching output.
