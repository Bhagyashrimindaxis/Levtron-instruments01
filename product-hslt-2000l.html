Hydrostatic Submersible Level Transmitter (Borewells, Tanks & Sumps)

The HSLT-2000L is a high-reliability submersible hydrostatic level transmitter designed for continuous depth and level measurement in deep borewells, rivers, water reservoirs, wastewater pits, and chemical holding tanks. Featuring a piezoresistive silicon pressure sensor with a flush 316L SS diaphragm and an atmospheric vent capillary cable, it delivers precision measurement (±0.5% FS) from 0-1m up to 0-200m water column.

Wastewater & Sewage Sumps: Flush diaphragm prevents clogging in effluent collection pits, sludge basins, and lift stations.

Rivers, Canals & Open Channels: Environmental monitoring of flood stages, irrigation canals, and reservoir capacities.

Chemical & Fuel Storage: Compatible with non-corrosive chemicals, oils, and liquids compatible with SS316L.

Working Principle of Hydrostatic Submersible Level Transmitter (Borewells, Tanks & Sumps)

The HSLT-2000L operates on the hydrostatic pressure principle: P = ρ × g × h, where hydrostatic pressure (P) exerted by the liquid column is directly proportional to the liquid height (h) above the sensor.

The liquid pressure acts on the 316L stainless steel diaphragm, deflecting a piezoresistive silicon Wheatstone bridge sensor.

The internal ASIC circuit temperature-compensates and amplifies the micro-voltage signal into a linear 4-20mA analog output.
