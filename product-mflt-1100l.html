Float Level Transmitter

The MFLT-1100L is a continuous magnetic float level transmitter delivering continuous 4-20mA current loop or RS485 Modbus digital output for liquid storage tanks, chemical vessels, and fuel reservoirs.

Applications & Suitability

Liquid Storage Tanks: Continuous level monitoring in diesel, water, and chemical storage tanks.

Fuel & Oil Tanks: Precise inventory monitoring in generator fuel tanks and oil depots.

Chemical Batching: Continuous feedback to PLC / SCADA automation systems.

The MFLT-1100L is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Continuous 4-20mA 2-wire loop powered or RS485 Modbus output.
  • High resolution reed switch and resistor ladder chain (5mm / 10mm resolution).
  • SS316 / SS316L / PP construction for chemical compatibility.
  • Weatherproof IP66 / Flameproof Exd enclosure.

Working Principle of Float Level Transmitter

A magnetic float moves vertically along the guide tube with rising liquid level, actuating internal precision reed switches connected to a resistor divider network to produce continuous 4-20mA output.
