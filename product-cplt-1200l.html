Continuous Capacitance Level Transmitter (Liquids)

The CPLT-1200L is a high-accuracy continuous capacitance level transmitter designed for reliable level measurement of conductive and non-conductive liquids. Built with full PTFE insulation and integral microprocessor electronics, it converts level changes directly into a linear 4-20mA current output. Available with rigid probes for tanks up to 3 meters and flexible rope probes for deep vessels up to 30 meters.

Working Principle of Continuous Capacitance Level Transmitter (Liquids)

The CPLT-1200L utilizes the principle of variable electrical capacitance. The insulated sensing probe acts as one electrode of a capacitor, and the metallic tank wall (or a parallel ground reference tube) acts as the counter electrode.

As liquid rises in the vessel, the air dielectric (εr ≈ 1) between the electrodes is replaced by the higher dielectric constant of the liquid. This produces a linear increase in capacitance.

The integral high-frequency electronic circuit converts this capacitance change into a calibrated 4-20mA current loop proportional to liquid level.
