Diaphragm Level Switch (Rubber / Neoprene)

The RDLS-1400S is a pressure-activated mechanical diaphragm level switch for low and high level alarms in bins, silos, and chutes containing dry, free-flowing, non-abrasive powders and grains.

Applications & Suitability

Grain Bins & Feed Hoppers: Level monitoring in flour, grain, and feed storage.

Packaging Hoppers: High and low level alarm in bagging plants.

Sawdust & Wood Chips: Level detection in woodworking and biomass facilities.

The RDLS-1400S is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Mechanical pressure activation — requires zero external electrical power to operate.
  • Heavy-duty Neoprene / Nitrile rubber diaphragm.
  • Adjustable spring sensitivity for light to medium bulk densities.
  • SPDT microswitch output.

Working Principle of Diaphragm Level Switch (Rubber / Neoprene)

Rising bulk material exerts pressure against the flexible rubber diaphragm, which mechanically actuates a microswitch.
