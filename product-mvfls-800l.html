Miniature Vibrating Fork Level Switch

The MVFLS-800L is a miniature stainless steel tuning fork level switch for compact liquid tanks, pipe dry-run protection, and OEM machinery skids. With its short 40mm fork tines, it fits into small vessels and pipes down to 1 inch.

Applications & Suitability

Pipe Dry Run Alarm: Mounts into liquid supply pipes to prevent pump cavitating and running dry.

Compact OEM Skids: Level control in hydraulic power units, lube oil skids, and chillers.

Dosing & Mixing Tanks: Point level detection in small chemical batching tanks.

The MVFLS-800L is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Ultra-compact fork length (40mm) for pipe and small vessel installation.
  • All-stainless-steel construction with M12 connector or cable gland.
  • Solid state PNP/NPN or relay output.
  • Maintenance-free piezoelectric drive.

Working Principle of Miniature Vibrating Fork Level Switch

Piezoelectric crystal vibrates miniature fork. Liquid immersion dampens vibration, triggering switching.
