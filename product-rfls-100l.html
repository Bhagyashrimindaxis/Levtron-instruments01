Compact RF Admittance Level Switch (Liquids)

The RFLS-100L is a compact RF admittance point level switch engineered for liquid level detection in tanks, process vessels, and pipelines. Featuring an active coat-guard driven shield, it reliably ignores viscous liquid coating, foam, and sticky slurry build-up on the probe.

Applications & Suitability

Liquid Level Control: Accurate high and low point level monitoring in tanks containing water, oils, fuels, and chemicals.

Pump Dry Run Protection: Instant level detection in suction pipes and supply tanks to prevent costly pump damage.

Viscous & Sticky Media: Maintains accurate switching in syrups, paints, lubricants, glues, and wastewater.

Overfill Prevention: Safeguards storage tanks and mixing vessels against hazardous overflows.

Interface Detection: Detects boundary levels between immiscible liquids such as oil and water.

Working Principle of Compact RF Admittance Level Switch (Liquids)

The RFLS-100L measures admittance changes at radio frequencies when liquid contacts the probe.

The active guard electrode is driven at the same electrical potential and frequency as the sensing electrode. This creates an equipotential zone that blocks current leakage through sticky coatings or wet residue.

The internal electronics measure only the true sense-to-earth admittance when liquid rises to the setpoint level, instantly switching the DPDT relay.
