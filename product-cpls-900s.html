Capacitance Level Switch (Dry Bulk & Powders)

The CPLS-900S is an economical and rugged capacitance level switch designed for point level detection of dry bulk solids, powders, grains, and non-conductive materials. Built with adjustable sensitivity, it detects materials with dielectric constants down to 1.5.

Working Principle of Capacitance Level Switch (Dry Bulk & Powders)

Measures capacitance between the insulated probe electrode and the metallic vessel wall. Material contact increases capacitance, activating the relay.
