Multi-Point Float Level Switch

The MFLS-1300L is a multi-point magnetic float level switch that provides up to 6 independent switching levels along a single vertical guide stem for pump auto-start, auto-stop, low alarm, and high alarm control.

Applications & Suitability

Pump Automation: Low level cut-off and high level pump start in sump and water tanks.

Chemical Storage Tanks: Multiple alarm thresholds (Low-Low, Low, High, High-High).

Cooling Towers & Boilers: Multi-stage water level control.

The MFLS-1300L is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Up to 6 switching levels on a single stem.
  • Hermetically sealed reed switches isolated from process media.
  • Available in SS316, SS304, PP, and PVDF materials.
  • Weatherproof IP66 or Flameproof enclosure.

Working Principle of Multi-Point Float Level Switch

Magnetic floats ride up and down the stem, closing specific reed switch contacts as liquid reaches pre-set elevations.
