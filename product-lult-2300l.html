Working Principle of Non-Contact Ultrasonic Level Transmitter (Liquids & Open Channels)

The LULT-2300L ultrasonic transducer emits high-frequency acoustic sound pulses (20 kHz to 350 kHz) directed toward the liquid surface.

The sound waves travel through the air, reflect off the liquid surface, and return to the transducer sensor.

The microprocessor measures the round-trip transit time (t) and, knowing the speed of sound in air (c = 344 m/s at 20°C, temperature-compensated), calculates the exact distance (D = c × t / 2) and determines the liquid level.
