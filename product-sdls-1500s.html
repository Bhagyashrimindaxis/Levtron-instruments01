Stainless Steel Diaphragm Switch

The SDLS-1500S is a heavy-duty metallic stainless steel diaphragm level switch designed for hot, abrasive, and corrosive bulk solids, cement silos, clinker bins, and coal bunkers.

Applications & Suitability

Cement Silos & Clinker Bins: Level monitoring in hot, abrasive cement clinker and raw meal silos.

Coal Bunkers: Level detection in thermal power plant coal bunkers.

Hot Fly Ash Hoppers: High temperature ash level monitoring.

The SDLS-1500S is a reliable, maintenance-free level measurement solution for industrial process automation.

Features & Benefits

  • Metallic SS304 / SS316 diaphragm resists abrasion, tearing, and hot materials up to 200°C.
  • Mechanical pressure operation requires zero supply power.
  • Flush mounting onto bin walls.
  • Rugged IP65 cast aluminium enclosure.

Working Principle of Stainless Steel Diaphragm Switch

Material weight pushes against the stainless steel diaphragm, flexing an internal lever to trigger the microswitch.
